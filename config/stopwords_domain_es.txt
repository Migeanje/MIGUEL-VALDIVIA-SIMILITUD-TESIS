# Criteria. A lemma is listed only when it carries no topic, because it:
# - names the thesis genre: the document, its parts, aims and findings, or the type of
#   research (tesis, objetivo, resultado, cuantitativo);
# - is a verb that reports what the research did (permitir, obtener, analizar);
# - names the institution, or the place or the time of the study (universidad, arequipa, año);
# - or is a generic modifier or connector that spaCy's list lacks (principal, cuyo).
# Frequent words that can tell topics apart stay out, even the most common ones: análisis,
# estudio, metodología, implementación, diseño, proceso, sistema, trabajo, tiempo, costo,
# productividad, mantenimiento. When in doubt, a word stays out.
#
